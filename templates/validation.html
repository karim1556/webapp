Autonomic Response (Heart Rate & HRV)

Pain triggers acute sympathetic excitation, raising Heart Rate (77.8 → 94.9 BPM) and causing sharp parasympathetic withdrawal in HRV RMSSD (62.7 → 39.4 ms).

Biomechanical Response (EMG & Posture)

Residual muscle clenching manifests as an elevation in EMG RMS (73.5 → 164.3 ADC units) accompanied by compensatory postural guarding (15.9 → 41.0 score).

Respiratory Alterations (HX710B)

Pain episodes provoke tachypneic breathing (14.2 → 21.9 breaths/min) and shallower tidal volume (0.70 → 0.50 normalized depth proxy).

Bayesian Channel Reliability Layer

Hand-crafted prior multipliers (EMG: 1.25x, Posture: 1.20x, HRV: 1.15x) weight high-specificity biosignals before classification to suppress single-channel motion artifacts.

Strongest Pain Predictors

Vagal Suppression Metric

Closed-Loop Vibrotactile Feedback Transfer Mechanics

Proportional PWM intensity mapping and anti-habituation pulsed duty cycle waveform.

Download 300 DPI Figure

Dynamic Activation Threshold

Actuation remains in standby (0 PWM) until P(Pain) >= 0.15. This 15% threshold enables early therapeutic intervention before severe cortical pain circuits lock in.

Proportional PWM Mapping (160–255)

PWM output scales linearly from 160 (subthreshold mechanoreceptor floor) up to 255 (maximum stimulation ceil) proportionally matching estimated pain severity.

Pulsed Burst Modulation (400ms ON / 200ms OFF)

Periodic rest intervals prevent sensory receptor fatigue and tactile adaptation while ensuring long battery autonomy (>8.5 hours continuous use).

3-Tier End-to-End System Architecture & Dataflow

Wearable Sensing & Actuation → Embedded Edge Processing & ML Core → Cloud Telemetry & Web Platform.

Download 300 DPI Figure

Tier 1: Physical Sensing & Actuation

MPU-6050 6-DoF IMU, AD8232 biopotential front-end (residual EMG & ECG), HX710B 24-bit strain gauge, and IRLZ44N MOSFET driving ERM/LRA vibrotactile haptic motors.

Tier 2: Edge DSP & ML Engine

ESP32 dual-core Xtensa MCU running FreeRTOS with 200Hz sampling, Pan-Tompkins QRS detection, Bayesian prior fusion, and 200-tree dual Random Forest inference.

Tier 3: Telemetry & Clinical Portal

Supabase stores wearable telemetry and manages authentication. The Flask workspace displays received readings alongside a locally stored pain diary.
